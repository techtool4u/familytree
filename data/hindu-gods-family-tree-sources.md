# Hindu deities & epic dynasties: family tree sources

**Files:**
- `hindu-gods-family-tree.ged`: GEDCOM 5.5.1 with real source (SOUR) records. It opens in Gramps, MyHeritage, Ancestry, RootsMagic and similar programs.
- `hindu-gods-family-tree.json`: the same tree for Kindred (**⋯ → Import a tree**), with per-person source links under **Media**.

The tree has **263 people in 162 families**, and the focus person is Krishna. It includes the full Lord Rama tree, so the Solar and Lunar dynasties connect through Vaivasvata Manu.

## How to read the tree

- **These are mythological genealogies.** Different texts disagree, sometimes sharply. When they disagree I picked the most widely cited version, wrote the alternatives into the person's notes, and tagged them **Variant**: Lakshmi (daughter of Bhrigu, or born from the ocean), Saraswati (Brahma's consort, or his daughter), Kadru, Simhika, Devasena, Budha's mother, Ganesha's and Kartikeya's marriages, Ashokasundari as Nahusha's wife, and Yayati in the Ikshvaku line.
- **Lineage gaps.** Four placeholder people named `··· Yadava kings ···`, `··· Paurava kings ···` and so on stand in for long runs of kings that aren't listed: Yadu → Shurasena, Puru → Dushyanta, Bharata → Kuru, and Kuru → Pratipa. Relationship degrees that pass through these gaps are therefore not exact. For example, the app calls Krishna Rama's "9th cousin 25× removed"; the real count is larger.
- **Divine and legal fathers.** In the Mahabharata the Pandavas were begotten by gods: Yudhishthira by Yama (Dharma), Bhima by Vayu, Arjuna by Indra, and Nakula and Sahadeva by the Ashvins. They're linked to **Pandu** as their father, the dynastic reading, and each person's notes name the divine father. Karna is linked to **Surya** and Kunti. Dhritarashtra, Pandu and Vidura are linked to **Vyasa**, their biological father by niyoga; Vichitravirya, their legal father, is named in the notes. Hanuman is linked to **Vayu**, his aurasa father in the Valmiki Ramayana, with Kesari in the notes.
- **Rebirths and avatars** aren't modelled as parent links. Parvati as Sati reborn, Rama and Krishna as avatars of Vishnu, and Sita and Rukmini as avatars of Lakshmi are written in the notes and tags.
- **No dates.** Ages, timelines, the map and the Time machine have nothing to work with. The Tree, Fan, Wheel, filters (try the tags *Deity*, *Pandava*, *Naga*, *Variant*) and the Relationship finder all work.
